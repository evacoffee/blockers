# blockers
a small godot game of a tiny sprite navigating a world of darkness with only a flashlight and a spear

play here!! 


https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8e14672e-84dc-4afb-95d9-d01082439912


game made with godot and gdscript! I created the game's 15 levels, player systems, enemies, UI, combat mechanics, flashlight system, power switches, and achievement system using Godot's scene and scripting systems. I used separate scripts for systems such as the player, drones, HUD, levels, achievements, and game management. I also used signals to communicate between different parts of the game, such as when the player takes damage, dies, activates a power switch, or unlocks an achievement. I'm super excited for people to play this game and I hope you enjoy it!

# features
- 15 levels that get harder
- title screen with buttons for extras, levels, achievements, and settings
- a spear for fighting enemies
- flashlight system that drains energy while exploring dark areas
- health and flashlight energy systems
- power switches that must be activated to progress through certain levels
- vending machines that restore flashlight energy
- heart pickups that restore health
- regular and electric drone enemies with different challenges
- knockback and enemy death animations
- achievement system that tracks player accomplishments
- level progression and save system

# controls
- WASD /  Move
- space / Attack with spear
- e / interact with different objects
- q / Use the pothole ability
- esc / Pause
